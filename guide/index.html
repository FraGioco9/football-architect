The football world

Football Architect connects eight countries, sixteen domestic divisions and 320 clubs in one fictional football world. Each country has a first and second tier, with sixteen active clubs in each division.

The club registry also includes 64 inactive clubs. Catalogue identity and sporting participation are separate: an inactive club is not automatically a league participant.

Divisions

Divisions are the competitions in which clubs are organized within each country. Every country has two levels: a first division and a second division.

Each division is designed for 16 clubs. A division describes the level of competition; it does not define a club's identity.

Clubs

Clubs are the football teams in Football Architect. Each club has its own name, country and unique three-letter abbreviation.

Clubs are the teams that take part in divisions. Their identity remains separate from the division in which they compete.

Movement between tiers

Promotion and relegation connect the two domestic tiers. The planned rules distinguish direct places from playoff and playout places; consult competition Rules for the applicable format.

These are sporting rules of the world, not a promise that a public simulation currently computes promotion, relegation or tie-breakers.

Seasons and calendar

The world calendar models a 56-day cycle across eight weeks: twelve preseason days, domestic and international event markers, scheduled pauses and a transition day.

The two international cup finals have separate days. Calendar markers describe the season structure; they do not generate kickoff times, fixtures, scores or live results.

International cups

The Sovereign Cup and Federation Cup bring clubs together beyond national borders. Their designed path includes a league phase and knockout rounds, ending in separate finals.

International competition pages are evolving. Empty states mean that fixtures, standings or results have not been supplied; they must not be mistaken for completed games.

Statistics and history

Competition views separate current standings and fixtures from team and player statistics. History explores completed seasons, champions and records when authoritative archive data exists.

Unavailable seasons and missing statistics use explicit empty states rather than invented historical results.

Explore the app

Use Competitions to choose a division and explore its Overview, Standings, Fixtures, Stats and History. Club pages present individual identities; Calendar explains the shared season rhythm.

The Guide explains concepts rather than listing every club or displaying provisional development results. Navigation and available views may change as the app evolves.

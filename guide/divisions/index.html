A two-tier national system

Eight countries each have one top division and one second division. Every division has sixteen places, giving the domestic world 16 divisions and 256 active league places.

How the divisions are organized

The first division sits above the second division in each country. The tiers have independent standings and fixtures; club identity does not change with its league.

League format

The shared competition preview uses a double round-robin: each club meets the other 15 clubs twice, once at home and once away. That means 30 regular matchdays with eight games on each matchday, or 240 matches per division.

Official scoring and tie-breaker implementation is not defined by this shared preview contract. Promotion and relegation are explained separately and should not be inferred from fictional development standings.

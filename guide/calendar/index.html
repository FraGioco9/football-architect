Seasons and calendar

The world calendar models a 56-day cycle across eight weeks: twelve preseason days, domestic and international event markers, scheduled pauses and a transition day.

The two international cup finals have separate days. Calendar markers describe the season structure; they do not generate kickoff times, fixtures, scores or live results.

The season cycle

Football Architect uses a conceptual 56-day calendar organized into eight weeks. It is an ordered sporting timeline rather than a promise of real-world dates or kickoff times.

Preseason and domestic rounds

The cycle reserves twelve preseason days. Domestic leagues have 30 regular matchdays, with eight fixtures per division on each matchday in the shared preview model.

International calendar

Domestic and international event markers share one world timeline. The Sovereign Cup and Federation Cup reach their finals on separate days in the 56-day model.

Pauses and transitions

Planned pauses and the transition day provide space between stages. A marker only identifies a stage; it does not create played games, scores or archived results.

Calendar data and navigation

The Calendar view presents the available season structure. DEV controls may allow inspection of different stages, but do not constitute an authoritative live clock.

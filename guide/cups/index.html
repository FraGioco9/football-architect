Two international competitions

Sovereign Cup and Federation Cup each feature 24 clubs. They are separate tournaments sharing one international calendar framework.

League phase

Each club plays six matches in the first phase, scheduled across international slots I01–I06. This is a competition format, not a claim that current fixtures or results exist.

Knockout pathway

The designed pathway continues through two-legged playoffs (I07–I08), quarterfinals (I09–I10) and semifinals (I11–I12), followed by the finals in international slot I13.

How the world calendar fits

Thirteen international slots are woven between domestic rounds. Both finals belong to slot I13, but the 56-day world calendar places them on separate days.

Availability of competition data

Standings, fixtures, statistics and history appear only when source data is available. An empty state does not imply a match was played or that a champion has been crowned.

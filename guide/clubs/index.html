Clubs and identity

The catalogue contains 320 clubs across eight countries: 256 active and 64 inactive. Club identity is independent of division placement.

What defines a club

Every club has a canonical primary name, full name, compact name and three-letter abbreviation, plus a home city, nickname and an original description.

Colours and crests

Every club has two canonical identity colours. Primary and secondary HEX values must be preserved exactly in approved artwork. Crests are introduced individually; missing artwork uses a placeholder, not an invented badge.

City, ground and culture

The city and stadium name connect the club to a place. Descriptions and nicknames provide cultural context; they do not determine sporting results.

Participation and rivalries

Each country has 32 active clubs across two leagues and eight additional inactive catalogue clubs. Rivalries are part of club lore and must not be mistaken for automatic scheduled fixtures.

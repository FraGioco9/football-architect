Understanding statistics

Statistics show what has happened in the selected competition and season. Overview summarises matches played, total goals and goals per match; the latter is shown to two decimal places.

Teams and players

Team and player tabs show ranked Top 5 tables across seven categories, with Show all where a full table is available. An empty table indicates missing data, not zero performance.

Standings versus records

Standings and fixtures describe the selected season's current state. History documents completed seasons, champions, final standings and long-term records; these are different views with different data requirements.

Season and all-time history

History can distinguish individual seasons from all-time records, including team and player achievements, seasonal attack and defence, and the most wins in a season.

Data integrity

History must not invent a champion before a season is complete. If there are no completed seasons or no authoritative records, the page displays a clear empty state instead of fictional achievements.

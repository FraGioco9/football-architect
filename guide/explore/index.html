Find your way around

Football Architect separates an overview of the world from its competitions, teams, calendar and settings. Start with a competition or use the dashboard to choose where to go next.

Dashboard

The dashboard is an entry point to the app. It helps you navigate; it does not by itself confirm live scores or completed matches.

Competitions

Choose a domestic division or an available international cup. Competition pages expose Overview, Standings, Fixtures, Stats and History when the corresponding data exists.

Calendar

Explore the shared season timeline in Calendar. Calendar markers are a model of events, not real-world dates or invented match results.

Clubs and settings

The club directory helps you discover identities; club pages describe individual teams. Settings control supported app preferences such as the visual theme and language.

Understanding empty states

A blank or unavailable panel means the underlying information is not available. Development-only previews are not official history or public results.

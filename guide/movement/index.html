How clubs move between tiers

The domestic model connects the top and second division within each country. The sporting design distinguishes automatic places from end-of-season decisions.

Top division: relegation

Two clubs are assigned direct relegation places. The clubs finishing 13th and 14th enter a playout to determine one additional relegation place.

Second division: promotion

Two clubs are assigned direct promotion places. Clubs finishing 3rd through 6th contest a promotion playoff for one additional place. The final is hosted by the better-ranked finalist.

Designed rules versus current preview

These are approved competition-format rules, not a statement that public standings currently calculate final placements, outcomes or tie-breakers. Development preview results must not be treated as confirmed promotion or relegation.

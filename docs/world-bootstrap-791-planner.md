`stationaryAgeQuotas(total, entryWeights, hazards)`: 4 nonnegative entry weights (16–19); 24 conditional retirement hazards for reached ages 17–40 (last must be 1). Stationary expected active mass at age a = surviving mass at a−1 plus entry fraction at a. Integer counts use largest remainder (ties younger first). Assumes replacement of retirees, no other exits/entries and fixed headcount; not an economic or academy simulation. Actual lifecycle #779 and youth #586/#777 remain gates.

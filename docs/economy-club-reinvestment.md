# Club reinvestment and recurring EUR revenue — owner direction DRAFT

Owner objective: clubs reinvest all seasonal earnings on average; recurring platform commercial revenue from approximately 10% of youth activations paid directly in EUR. Match activations to retirements, 100% human-agent main scenario, EUR90% of displayed value and ARC100% research reference; unsold costly assets deferred. This is an economic target, not a mandatory budget/spending rule or a guarantee of demand. Allow club buffers and uneven seasonal spending. No change to runtime rules or prices.

World club reinvestment includes payroll and actual sporting spend; interclub transfers redistribute money and cannot close the reserve cycle. Staff remains inside operating surplus until actual expenditure. Only confirmed reserve credits are recycling. Existing v4 candidate (3.12M Rewards, K150, 332 activations including 33 EUR) yields 1,506,971 payroll, 1,340,836 youth ARC receipts and 1,613,029 net club cash before discretionary spending. Even if every remaining club ARC were paid to reserve, receipts are 2,953,865 ARC; 166,135 ARC remain with agents. Full reserve closure needs equivalent actual agent reserve receipts or another reviewed adjustment, not assumed EUR conversion. At fixed 299 ARC purchases, full payroll recovery from youth alone needs mean ARC price 5,041 (vs reference 4,484), and cannot be guaranteed by raising prices. EUR receipts are EUR1,331.87 gross at 33 orders; actual profit subtracts real costs, processing, tax/refunds and overhead.

Illustrative target sensitivities below keep the observed-in-model v4 youth-receipt/payroll ratio constant, **not** an independently recalibrated salary/market value or license forecast. Staff/facility/service spend remains undefined, no mandatory fees or automatic drains. At 75% payroll, 2.34M payroll and 780k club spending directly to reserve, youth would recover about 2.082M ARC under that ratio; residual agent receipts still needed about 258k. All club spend to reserve is an optimistic condition, not verified user behavior. Recalibrate licenses (EUR300/100) and actual spending/affordability before approval; no change to 3x valuation multiple, reward scale, actual wage or youth rates. Positive standardized surplus for license calculation is pre-discretionary, not perpetual net cash savings. No cash redemption or double currency charge.

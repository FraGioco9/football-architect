Owner selected paid initial mandates, no free allocation. 6400 synthetic opening players aged 16–32, 256 active canonical clubs, 256 human buyer wallets initially zero, all opening players AI/unrepresented. 2.4M seasonal prizes candidate, guarded opening and renewal contracts, K214, +10% raw market value, youth99% ARC/90% EUR, secondary executed fee5%, no staff. Primary purchase changes mandate only, not sporting status or contract, never creates player or ARC; historical earned salary remains attributed to prior recipient. Unsold existing players continue playing under AI and may retire unsold. Youth activation cap remains population deficit. Primary inventory finite, initial 6400; no repeated primary sale. No actual player snapshot or observed user behavior.

15 diagnostic paths of 100 seasons: three seeds for each of three demand scenarios with dual99/90 existing mandate quote; three seeds each for EUR-only90 and dual90/80 alternative under reference demand only. Full 27 path grid exceeded tool execution time; alternatives deliberately restricted to reference. Existing mandate prices/currency not approved by this research. Quotes use current public age/skill forecast, upward rounding; no private retirement age. Existing mandates sampled before payroll, up to two shopping attempts per agent per year with same assumed EUR budget shared with later youth acquisition; youth has up to three attempts. Random sample of affordable options, preference only when both currencies affordable, no forced purchase or currency quota. Primary purchase order and demand proxy are assumptions, not real willingness to pay. No EUR cash receipts converted into ARC. Paid AI wages credit reserve, subsequent human wages credit wallet. No free replacements. Reference EUR budget EUR250/person/season, low EUR50, high EUR500. Prior reference proxy parameters otherwise unchanged.

Reserve checks reconstruct exact no-issuance prefix of reserve-independent diagnostic trace, stopping before first unfunded full prize pool. Diagnostic injections merely measure later hypothetical paths, not authorized funding. All 15 diagnostic paths have zero final and peak season-end arrears, 6400 final population. No structural zero-issuance proof.

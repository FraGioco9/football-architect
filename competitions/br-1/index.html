    International sporting outcomes

    1–3Sovereign Cup qualification (subject to titleholder allocation)
    4–6Federation Cup qualification (subject to titleholder allocation)

    International qualification (next season): normally domestic places 1–3 enter the Sovereign Cup and places 4–6 the Federation Cup. The defending Sovereign champion retains a Sovereign place; the defending Federation champion moves to Sovereign. Both use one of their country’s three Sovereign places, with remaining places filled by the highest-ranked eligible clubs. A relegated defending champion remains eligible.

    Relegation play-out

    Two legs. The club with the better original table rank hosts the return leg; the other club hosts first. A tie winner keeps its own original rank. Aggregate score; no away-goals rule. If level after the return leg, extra time and then penalties decide the winner.

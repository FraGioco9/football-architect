    Competition format

    Each division has 16 clubs. Every club plays the other 15 twice, once at home and once away. The league consists of 30 matchdays and 240 matches.

    League ranking

    Clubs are ranked by league points. Ties are resolved by a head-to-head mini-table using points, goal difference and goals scored; the criteria are reapplied to any subset still tied. If needed, overall goal difference, overall goals scored and total league wins follow. A remaining tie is decided by an auditable and reproducible draw.

    Sporting outcomes

    1National champion
    2–12Remain in First Division

    15–16Direct relegation

    Relegation play-out

    The 13th- and 14th-placed clubs meet over two legs: the 14th-placed club hosts the first leg and the 13th-placed club hosts the return leg. The aggregate winner remains in the First Division and the loser is relegated. If aggregate scores are level after the second leg, extra time and then penalties decide the winner.

    Competition format

    Each division has 16 clubs. Every club plays the other 15 twice, once at home and once away. The league consists of 30 matchdays and 240 matches.

    League ranking

    Clubs are ranked by league points. Ties are resolved by a head-to-head mini-table using points, goal difference and goals scored; the criteria are reapplied to any subset still tied. If needed, overall goal difference, overall goals scored and total league wins follow. A remaining tie is decided by an auditable and reproducible draw.

    Sporting outcomes

    1–2Direct promotion
    3–6Promotion play-offs
    7–16Remain in Second Division

    The Second Division is the lowest active national tier. Clubs do not drop to a Third Division.

    Promotion play-offs

    The semi-finals are 3rd vs 6th and 4th vs 5th, each played over two legs with the higher-ranked club at home in the return leg. The winners meet in a single-match final hosted by the finalist with the better regular-season ranking. The final winner earns promotion. No points-gap threshold applies. Tied aggregate semi-finals or a tied final go to extra time and then penalties if necessary.
